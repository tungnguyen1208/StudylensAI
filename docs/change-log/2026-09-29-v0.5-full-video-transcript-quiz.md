# Change: Full transcript to full-video quiz

- Code: `2026-09-29-v0.5-full-video-transcript-quiz`
- Date: `2026-09-29`
- Version: `0.5.0`
- Status: `accepted`

## Reason for change

Periodic watched-time segments delayed quiz delivery and made the session state
depend on a timer instead of the available learning material. The product now
needs one complete transcript and one quiz for the whole YouTube video.

## Business behavior: before and after

Before, an accepted caption capture enabled a session and the client repeatedly
created watched-time segments for 5/10/15 minute quiz cycles. After, ON on a
supported video immediately creates a Backend session. The Extension uploads
one timestamped full transcript; Backend validates it, invokes a public-video
Gemini fallback only when direct captions are unavailable or insufficient, and
generates one full-video quiz. The learner submits all answers together and
only then receives answer, explanation, and evidence timestamps.

## Architecture decision and rejected alternatives

The preferred transcript source remains YouTube `captionTracks`, Timedtext
JSON3, XML, then DOM fallback. Backend remains the source of truth for
sessions, transcripts, processing jobs, quizzes, attempts, and history.
FastAPI is a stateless AI boundary; its public-URL transcript generation is
strictly a Backend-requested fallback for public videos.

Rejected: browser audio/STT, Extension-to-provider calls, timer/segment quiz
generation, a separate unofficial transcript backend, and a vector/RAG store.

## Affected contracts, data, and modules

- Public Session Quiz API: immediate session, full transcript PUT,
  learning-package GET, retry, complete.
- Public Assessment API: one batch quiz-attempt POST.
- AI API: full-transcript quiz generation and public-video transcript fallback.
- Extension: single full transcript upload, polling/recovery, complete quiz
  display, batch submit, evidence seeking.
- SQLite: videos, study sessions, processing jobs, question options, quiz
  attempts, and attempt answers. Existing audio capture and single-answer rows
  remain legacy readable data.

## Compatibility and migration

The baseline is `0.5.0`. The migration adds the full-video entities without
deleting legacy audio or answer data. Old capture/segment HTTP paths are no
longer mapped in runtime; the session endpoint is the only public transcript
ingestion path.

## Risks and limits

Gemini fallback only works for public YouTube videos and depends on the
configured provider. Private or unlisted videos stay unavailable. Jobs retry at
most three times and expose their last error to the side panel. Long transcripts
are chunked by FastAPI; quiz count is bounded from 3 to 15.

## Acceptance criteria and test evidence

Expected evidence before status becomes `implemented`:

- Extension caption/fallback/stale-transition tests, typecheck and build.
- Contract tests for session, assessment, and activation boundaries.
- Backend tests for immediate sessions, transcript validation/idempotency,
  fallback job queue, batch attempts, migration and recovery.
- FastAPI transcript, structured output, timeout, and long-transcript tests.
- Chrome/Edge smoke for caption/manual/ASR/no-caption, A-to-B, OFF, and
  Backend/AI outage.

Automated evidence is filled in when the final verification command set passes.

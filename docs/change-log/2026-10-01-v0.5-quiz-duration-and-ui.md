# Change: Duration-based question targets and readable quiz UI

- Code: `2026-10-01-v0.5-quiz-duration-and-ui`
- Date: `2026-10-01`
- Version: `0.5.0` (API shape unchanged)
- Status: `implemented`

## Reason for change

The old five-minute ceiling selected too few questions for 10–30 minute videos and did not match the desired 30/60-minute bands. In the side panel, a broad input rule stretched radio controls to full width, separating each option from its label. The AI chunk size also exceeded the prompt's evidence cap, so a long chunk could silently omit later cues.

## Business behavior: before and after

- Before: Backend requested `clamp(3, 15, ceil(videoEndMs / 300000))` questions. Quiz choices were cramped and radio controls could appear above their text.
- After: one full-video quiz targets 3 questions below 10 minutes, 5 from 10 to below 30, 10 from 30 to below 60, and 15 from 60 minutes onward. Exact boundaries enter the higher band. The stored player duration selects the target; the final cue timestamp is used when duration is unavailable. A cue extending beyond the player duration remains inside the AI evidence range without inflating the target.
- The target is not a requirement to invent content. A nonempty, validated response with fewer grounded questions remains publishable, and the side panel displays the actual number. No periodic quiz timer is introduced.

## Architecture decision and rejected alternatives

- Backend owns the duration policy and caps a returned quiz at its requested target. FastAPI shares one 24,000-character text budget between chunking and prompt construction, includes every cue in its assigned chunk, deduplicates questions, and selects over-target results across the full timeline. An individual evidence chunk may produce zero questions; the final public quiz must still contain at least one validated question.
- The Extension renders the existing public quiz contract using native keyboard-operable radio controls, whole-row labels, theme tokens, and larger text. Correct answers and explanations remain hidden until submission.
- Rejected: a new quiz per 30 minutes watched, browser-side question generation, a contract version bump, a database migration, or fabricated questions to satisfy a target.

## Affected contracts, data, and modules

- The Backend-to-AI `questionCount` contract description and its 15-minute request example now describe a target of 5; request/response fields and `0.5.0` limits remain unchanged.
- Backend Session Quiz worker, FastAPI question-generation prompt/service, and Extension Assessment History presentation are affected. The shared/HOT `sidepanel.css` changes only quiz presentation. No persisted schema changes.

## Compatibility and migration

Existing quizzes and attempts keep their original question counts and results. Only new quiz-generation jobs after service restart use the new duration policy. Backend, AI Service, and unpacked Extension must be restarted/reloaded to use their respective changes. Existing public clients remain compatible.

## Risks and limits

- Gemini may return fewer grounded questions than requested; a nonempty result is accepted, while an empty result remains an error. Provider quota and long-video processing time still apply.
- A single unusually long caption cue can exceed the normal per-chunk text budget because it is not silently truncated or assigned fabricated sub-cue timestamps.
- A live Chrome side-panel screenshot and a new end-to-end quiz after restarting the running services/Extension remain manual verification items. Browser-tab capture could not capture Chrome's native side panel.
